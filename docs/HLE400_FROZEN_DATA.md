| arm | P_act | P_flip (final) | EIR (num/den) | ECR (num/den) | wrong→correct switches | correct→wrong switches | tasks with ≥1 switch | mean rounds |

Definitions: EIR = P(correct→wrong) per round over all rounds whose previous state was correct; ECR likewise
from wrong. P_act = correct answer committed at least once; P_flip = P_act and final wrong.
Stability condition `ECR/EIR > Acc/(1−Acc)` with Acc = 0.2825 → threshold 0.39: **C3 0.38 (at the boundary)**,
A2 0.60 (inside). The critic triples the activation of A2 (190 vs 65 wrong→correct switches) but also
triples EIR (0.298 vs 0.097) — exactly the quantity the evaluator must suppress.
