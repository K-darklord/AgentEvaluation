# Cover note for review

**Draft of 11 November 2026. Working title:** *Where the Gains of Self-Critique Come From: Iteration Selects, Critique Reaches, and Same-Model Judges Add Nothing Detectable*

**Intended venue:** ACL 2027 main conference via ACL Rolling Review (deadline 4 January 2027).

## What the paper claims

A self-critique loop (one model answers, criticizes itself, answers again) gains in two separable ways, and placebo controls attribute each to a different source.

1. **Selection comes largely from iteration.** A loop that is only reassured, voted over its own rounds, selects as many correct answers as nine-sample self-consistency at half the tokens. A real critic adds about 12 items on top, which is not significant at 400 items.
2. **Reach comes from critique.** The number of items whose trajectory contains the correct answer rises from 153 (reassurance) to 171 (dose-matched templated suspicions that read nothing) to 198 (generated critique). A vocabulary distilled from the model's past errors adds nothing.
3. **The model cannot convert the extra reach itself.** Last answers are no better than under a placebo, and three same-model judges are at chance wherever they disagree with the vote count.

## Status of the evidence

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Ceiling: critique > answer-blind pressure > reassurance | Firm. Each step p ≤ 0.005; holds at matched candidate counts and under exact-match scoring only. |
| Free critic selects more than self-consistency | Holds (+20.7, 95% interval [9.0, 32.4]). At least +13 under a conservative bound for vote splitting. A cost-matched comparison (eleven samples) and a universal self-consistency baseline are run and await scoring. |
| Placebo loop ≥ self-consistency at half the cost | Direction consistent (+8 to +9 items), not significant. |
| Critic selects more than the placebos | Suggestive only (+11 to +12 items, p = 0.07 to 0.08). |
| Error vocabulary adds nothing | Firm as a null at this sample size. |
| Same-model judges add nothing to the count | Holds on one model and one condition; gains of a few items cannot be excluded. |

## Known weaknesses

- One model (deepseek-v4-flash), one benchmark (Humanity's Last Exam, 400 items), one run per condition. A replication on a second model (qwen3.8-flash, same 400 items, pre-specified tests) is running.
- The critic costs about 2.5 times the placebo loop and 1.2 times nine-sample self-consistency.
- The controls do not separate "the critique reads the answer" from "the critique is generated by the model".
- Free-form answers are scored with a model-based tier that has not yet been audited by human annotators. The ceiling result holds without that tier.
- Pre-specification was recorded internally, not registered externally. The original falsification test was written for the bank critic; the clearer result belongs to the free critic, which was introduced as a control. The manuscript says so.
- Section 6 (same-model judges) still uses trajectories recorded before three items were rerun; it will be recomputed.
- No figures yet; the appendix is an outline; references beyond the five checked against sources need verification.

4. Is the same-model judge study (Section 6) better kept in this paper or separated?
